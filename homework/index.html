Fix-It Zone

🛠️ Slow, careful repairs

Average rateReview
Problem: Ajay drove 450 miles in 7 hours. Find the average speed, rounded to the nearest whole number.
Common trap
Stopping at 64 without checking the decimal or rounding rule.
Complete reasoning
450 ÷ 7 = 64.285... → 64 mph.
Step 1: Average speed means distance ÷ time. Step 2: Compute 450 ÷ 7. Step 3: Look at the tenths digit. Since 64.285... has a 2 in the tenths place, it rounds down to 64. Step 4: Add the unit, miles per hour.
Money divisionFocus
Problem: $20.60 ÷ 16.
What makes it tricky
The decimal must stay aligned, and money answers need two decimal places.
Correct answer
$20.60 ÷ 16 = $1.2875, which is $1.29 to the nearest cent.
Think first: 16 × $1 = $16, so the answer should be a little more than $1, not $12 or $0.12. Then divide carefully. Finally round to the nearest cent because money is normally reported to two decimal places.
Context + remainderKeep strong
Example: 82 people need buses that hold 32 people each.
Incomplete interpretation
82 ÷ 32 = 2 remainder 18, so “2 buses.”
Correct interpretation
3 buses, because the remaining 18 people still need a bus.
Sarah got this kind of problem right. Keep the habit: the remainder changes meaning depending on the story.
